Main track · Oral

        

          
CONGA: Continual Neural Gated Architecture for Long-History Sequential Recommendation

          
Hoang-Vu Le*, Tuong-Bach-Hy Nguyen*, Bac Le · *equal contribution · Minneapolis, USA · Sep 2026 · pp. 125–134 · open access (CC BY)

          
Diagnoses two structural failure modes of sequential recommenders on long histories — positional degradation and hard context limits — and resolves them with length-robust encoding plus neural long-term memory. State of the art on 14 of 16 accuracy metrics across four benchmarks, up to +18.7% HR@5, with custom CUDA kernels giving a 10× faster training pipeline.

RET ’26Residual-stream theory

        

          
Not All Residuals Are Equal: Doubly-Stochastic Mixing Guarantees Balanced Gradient Flow in Multi-Stream Transformers

          
Hoang-Vu Le, Tuong-Bach-Hy Nguyen, Huu-Sang Le, Tien-Dat Truong, Bac Le · 2026

          
Proves that doubly-stochastic mixing is necessary and sufficient for balanced gradient flow across parallel residual streams, grounds the result in Riemannian geometry, and validates it on four sequential-recommendation benchmarks.
